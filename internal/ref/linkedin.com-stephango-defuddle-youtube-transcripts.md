The YouTube transcript feature is the one I didn't know I needed. I watch probably 2-3 hours of long-form interviews a week — founders, health researchers, the occasional jiu-jitsu breakdown — and the amount of insight that just evaporates because I didn't write it down is criminal. Being able to paste a link and get a searchable, chaptered transcript in markdown changes the whole workflow. Obsidian keeps quietly shipping tools that actually respect how people think. Appreciate the craft here.
